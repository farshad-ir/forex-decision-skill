# Decision Framework

## Core Principle

Observers do not predict the future with certainty.

Every market situation may lead to one of three outcomes:

* Up
* Down
* Range

The role of observers is to provide evidence, not certainty.

---

## Market Context

Before any trading decision, the market context must be understood.

Examples:

* Previous major move was upward.
* Previous major move was downward.
* Previous major move was range.

A major move is a meaningful market movement, typically much larger than local noise.

Observers should always be interpreted within context.

---

## Observer Layer

Observers collect evidence from the chart.

Current observers include:

* Structure
* Small Box
* Large Box
* Rainbow
* TraceSlope
* Dynamo
* STC

Observers report market conditions.

Observers do not trade.

---

## Scenario Layer

After reviewing observers and market history, three scenarios are considered:

### Scenario A

Market moves higher.

### Scenario B

Market moves lower.

### Scenario C

Market remains in range.

All three scenarios remain possible.

The objective is not to eliminate uncertainty.

The objective is to estimate which scenario currently has stronger evidence.

---

## Structure Layer

Structure provides important trading references.

Examples:

* Important highs
* Important lows
* Breakout points
* Inside points

Structure helps define:

* Stop Loss
* Take Profit
* Trade invalidation

---

## Price Action Layer

Price action is the final judge before entry.

Observers may suggest a bullish or bearish scenario.

Price action determines whether execution is justified.

Questions:

* Is the market accelerating?
* Is the market stalling?
* Is the market rejecting a level?
* Is momentum increasing or decreasing?

Price action may confirm or reject an observer-based hypothesis.

---

## Risk / Reward Layer

Even a correct scenario does not guarantee a good trade.

Before execution:

* Risk must be known.
* Reward must be estimated.
* R/R must be acceptable.

A trade with poor R/R should be rejected.

No trade is a valid decision.

---

## Execution Layer

Possible actions:

* Buy
* Sell
* Wait

Waiting is a valid outcome.

The objective is not constant participation.

The objective is long-term survival and profitability.

---

## Trading Philosophy

Indicators do not trade.

Decision skill trades.

Observers provide evidence.

The trader evaluates scenarios.

Price action provides confirmation.

Risk management protects capital.

The final decision belongs to the decision engine.
